Master Thesis Research Project

Do "Prime" Real Estate Funds Outperform Their Conventional Counterparts?
An empirical study of US-listed Office REITs, 2006–2024.

Project Overview

This research project, conducted as part of a Master's thesis at the University of St. Gallen (HSG), examines whether US-listed Office REITs with higher-quality physical assets generate superior risk-adjusted returns relative to their conventional counterparts. The study extends the frameworks of Eichholtz, Kok and Yonder (2012) and Ahlfeldt and Pietrostefani (2022).

Methodology

The analysis constructs a Composite Primeness Score across three dimensions — Function, Form, and Urban Design — using property-level data on approximately 1,706 office assets held by 99 REITs over the fiscal years 2008 through 2024. Urban Design indicators include walkability and location-quality measures based on the EPA Smart Location Database and third-party walkability scores.

Data Sources

The project draws on property and entity data from S&P Capital IQ Pro (SNL Real Estate), the EPA Smart Location Database, and supplementary walkability indicators from publicly available APIs.
